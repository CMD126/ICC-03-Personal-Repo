# Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)

Frameworks, risk management processes, and compliance concepts used in cybersecurity programs.

---

## Notes

### Key Frameworks

| Framework | Focus |
|-----------|-------|
| NIST CSF | Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover |
| ISO 27001 | Information security management system (ISMS) |
| GDPR | EU data privacy regulation |
| HIPAA | US healthcare data protection |
| PCI-DSS | Payment card industry security |
| SOC 2 | Service organization controls (Trust Services) |

### Risk Management

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| Risk | Likelihood × Impact of a threat event |
| Threat | Potential cause of harm |
| Vulnerability | Weakness that can be exploited |
| Asset | Anything of value to the organization |
| Control | Measure that reduces risk |

### Risk Response Strategies

| Strategy | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Accept | Risk within tolerance — do nothing |
| Mitigate | Apply controls to reduce risk |
| Transfer | Insurance, outsourcing |
| Avoid | Eliminate the activity causing the risk |
