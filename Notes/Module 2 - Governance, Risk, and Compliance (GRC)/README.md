# Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) Cheat Sheet

A reference for key GRC frameworks, risk concepts, and compliance standards used in cybersecurity.

---

## Key Frameworks

| Framework | Full Name | Purpose |
|-----------|-----------|---------|
| **NIST CSF** | Cybersecurity Framework | Voluntary framework of standards and best practices to manage cybersecurity risk. Core functions: Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, Recover. |
| **NIST SP 800-53** | Security and Privacy Controls | Catalog of security controls for federal information systems. |
| **ISO/IEC 27001** | Information Security Management | International standard for establishing and managing an ISMS (Information Security Management System). |
| **SOC 2** | Service Organization Control 2 | Audit framework for service providers covering security, availability, confidentiality, integrity, and privacy. |
| **PCI-DSS** | Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard | Requirements for organizations that handle credit/debit card data. |
| **HIPAA** | Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act | US law protecting patient health information (PHI). |
| **GDPR** | General Data Protection Regulation | EU regulation governing personal data collection, storage, and processing. |
| **CIS Controls** | Center for Internet Security Controls | Prioritized set of actions to defend against common cyber attacks. |

---

## Risk Management

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Risk** | The potential for loss or damage when a threat exploits a vulnerability. |
| **Threat** | Any circumstance or event with the potential to harm an asset. |
| **Vulnerability** | A weakness that can be exploited by a threat. |
| **Asset** | Anything of value to the organization (data, systems, people). |
| **Likelihood** | Probability that a threat will exploit a vulnerability. |
| **Impact** | The consequence or damage resulting from a successful attack. |
| **Risk = Likelihood × Impact** | Basic risk calculation formula. |
| **Residual Risk** | Risk remaining after controls are applied. |
| **Risk Appetite** | The level of risk an organization is willing to accept. |
| **Risk Tolerance** | The acceptable deviation from risk appetite. |

---

## Risk Response Strategies

| Strategy | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Avoid** | Eliminate the activity that introduces the risk. |
| **Mitigate** | Implement controls to reduce likelihood or impact. |
| **Transfer** | Shift risk to a third party (e.g., insurance, outsourcing). |
| **Accept** | Acknowledge the risk and take no further action (documented). |

---

## Governance Concepts

| Concept | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Policy** | High-level statement of organizational intent and direction. |
| **Standard** | Mandatory requirements that support a policy. |
| **Guideline** | Recommended (non-mandatory) best practices. |
| **Procedure** | Step-by-step instructions for performing a task. |
| **Audit** | Independent review to verify compliance with policies and standards. |
| **Due Diligence** | Investigating and understanding risks before acting. |
| **Due Care** | Taking reasonable steps to protect assets after identifying risks. |

---

## Compliance Key Terms

| Term | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Compliance** | Adherence to laws, regulations, standards, and policies. |
| **Regulatory Requirement** | Legally mandated rules (e.g., HIPAA, GDPR, PCI-DSS). |
| **Audit Trail** | Chronological record of system/user activity for accountability. |
| **Data Retention** | How long data must be stored per legal/regulatory requirements. |
| **BIA** | Business Impact Analysis — identifies critical processes and the effect of disruptions. |
| **BCP** | Business Continuity Plan — plan to maintain operations during disruptions. |
| **DRP** | Disaster Recovery Plan — procedures to recover IT systems after an incident. |
| **RTO** | Recovery Time Objective — maximum acceptable downtime. |
| **RPO** | Recovery Point Objective — maximum acceptable data loss (by time). |
